FDE platform engineering wall

Dense reference sheets, not slogan posters. Six tabloid (17x11) landscape panels. Hang left to right (1 to 6). Each of the 14 steps has Say, Ask, Platform box, Fail if skipped, and Deterministic bias. Readable at arm's length.

How to read a card

  • Say: talk track an FDE actually speaks
  • Ask: discovery and architecture questions
  • Platform box: artifact you leave behind
  • Fail if skipped: enterprise failure mode
  • Deterministic bias: when this step prefers rules, workflow, or allowlist
